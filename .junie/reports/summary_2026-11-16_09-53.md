   - Fix bugs and typos such as `payload.timeYeahstamp` and Leaflet marker type errors.
   - Introduce strict TypeScript definitions in `src/types/` for OSM elements, changesets, Nominatim results, Schema.org places, tag diffs, and messaging payloads.
   - Set up Vitest test runner with JSDOM and `@testing-library/svelte`.
